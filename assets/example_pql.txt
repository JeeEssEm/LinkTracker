RED метрики

Rate:

sum(rate(http_server_request_duration_seconds_count{service_name=~"$app"}[5m])) by (service_name)

Errors:
(sum(rate(http_server_request_duration_seconds_count{service_name=~"$app", http_response_status_code=~"5.."}[$__rate_interval])) or sum(rate(http_server_request_duration_seconds_count{service_name=~"$app"}[$__rate_interval])) * 0) / sum(rate(http_server_request_duration_seconds_count{service_name=~"$app"}[$__rate_interval])) * 100

(sum(rate(http_server_request_duration_seconds_count{service_name=~"$app", http_response_status_code=~"4.."}[$__rate_interval])) or sum(rate(http_server_request_duration_seconds_count{service_name=~"$app"}[$__rate_interval])) * 0) / sum(rate(http_server_request_duration_seconds_count{service_name=~"$app"}[$__rate_interval])) * 100

Duration:
histogram_quantile(0.99, sum(rate(http_server_request_duration_seconds_bucket{service_name=~"$app"}[5m])) by (le, app))
histogram_quantile(0.95, sum(rate(http_server_request_duration_seconds_bucket{service_name=~"$app"}[5m])) by (le, app))
histogram_quantile(0.50, sum(rate(http_server_request_duration_seconds_bucket{service_name=~"$app"}[5m])) by (le, app))


RAM:
sum(dotnet_process_memory_working_set_bytes{service_name=~"$app"}) by (app)


Бизнесовые метрики

Общее число команд в боте:
sum(command_requests_total)

Общее число отправленных уведомлений:
sent_notification_total

Запросы во внешние сервисы (из бота):
histogram_quantile(0.99, sum by(le, scope, scope_type) (rate(command_duration_milliseconds_bucket[5m])))
histogram_quantile(0.95, sum by(le, scope, scope_type) (rate(command_duration_milliseconds_bucket[5m])))
histogram_quantile(0.5, sum by(le, scope, scope_type) (rate(command_duration_milliseconds_bucket[5m])))

Время работы команды:
sum by(command) (rate(command_requests_total[5m]))
sum(rate(command_requests_total[5m]))

Количество ссылок с разбивкой по типу:
links_on_track
